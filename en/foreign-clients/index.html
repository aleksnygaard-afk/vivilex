Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ for Foreign Clients

Can I get advice from abroad?

Yes. Consultations can take place via WhatsApp, email, or video call, and documents can be sent electronically.

Do foreign documents need to be legalised?

Foreign documents to be used in Indonesia generally need to be legalised or apostilled, and then translated by a sworn translator. We will tell you which documents require this.

In which language are court hearings held?

Hearings in Indonesian courts are conducted in Indonesian. We represent you in court and explain every step to you in English or Norwegian.

Can a foreigner own property in their own name?

Foreigners cannot hold Freehold Title (Hak Milik), but may own a residential home under a Right of Use (Hak Pakai) in accordance with the regulations. Avoid nominee arrangements (registering property in someone else's name), as they are high-risk and may be void by law.
