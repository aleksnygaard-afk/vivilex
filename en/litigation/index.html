Frequently Asked Questions

Litigation FAQ

What is the difference between breach of contract and an unlawful act?

Breach of contract (wanprestasi) is a failure to meet obligations under an agreement. An unlawful act (perbuatan melawan hukum) covers conduct that breaks the law and causes loss, without the need for an agreement. The basis of the claim affects what must be proven.

What is a small claims lawsuit?

A small claims lawsuit (gugatan sederhana) is a fast-track procedure for breach of contract or unlawful act disputes with a claim value of up to IDR 500 million. The case is heard by a single judge with a short resolution period.

Must every civil claim go through mediation?

Generally, yes. Before the merits are examined, the parties must attempt mediation in court. A mediated settlement can be confirmed as a deed of settlement.

How long does a civil case take?

It depends on the complexity of the case and whether there is an appeal or cassation. We give you a realistic time estimate after reviewing your case.

Can a foreign party sue or be sued in Batam?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can be parties to cases in Indonesian courts. Hearings are held in Indonesian; we explain every step to you in English or Norwegian.
