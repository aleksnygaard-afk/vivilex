Not every document needs to be complete at the first consultation. We will tell you what is needed for your case.

  • ID card or passport of both parties
  • Marriage book or marriage certificate

  • Proof of ownership of marital assets (certificates, vehicle titles, bank accounts)
  • For inheritance: death certificate and details of all heirs
  • Marriage agreement, if any
Frequently Asked Questions

Family Law FAQ

Which court handles a divorce?

For Muslim couples, divorce is filed with the Religious Court. For non-Muslim couples, it is filed with the District Court. Batam has its own first-instance court of each kind.

Is mediation mandatory in divorce cases?

Yes. Before the merits are examined, the court requires the parties to attempt mediation. Mediation can also lead to agreement on child custody and division of property.

Can a separation-of-property agreement be made after marriage?

Yes. Since Constitutional Court Decision No. 69/PUU-XIII/2015, marriage agreements can be made before or during the marriage. This is especially important for mixed-nationality couples who want to own property in Indonesia.

Which law applies to the division of an estate?

It depends on the religion of the deceased and the heirs. Generally, either Islamic inheritance law (Compilation of Islamic Law) or civil inheritance law (Civil Code) applies. We help determine the applicable law and calculate each heir's share.

How much does a divorce lawyer cost?

Fees depend on the type and complexity of the case, for example whether custody or property is disputed. We give you a written fee breakdown before taking on the case.
