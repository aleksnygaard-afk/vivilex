Preparation

To set up a PT, prepare

The exact requirements depend on the type of business and whether there are foreign shareholders.

  • Proposed company names (several alternatives)
  • ID card (KTP) and tax number (NPWP) of Indonesian shareholders, directors, and commissioners
  • Passports of foreign shareholders or officers
  • Parent company documents, if a shareholder is a legal entity
  • Registered office address
  • Description of business activities and capital plan
Frequently Asked Questions

Corporate Law FAQ

What is the difference between a regular PT and a PT PMA?

A PT PMA is a company that is partly or wholly owned by foreign parties. It is subject to foreign investment rules, including which business sectors are open to foreign ownership and requirements for investment value and capital.

What is a KBLI code and why does it matter?

KBLI is Indonesia's business activity classification code. It determines which licences are required, the business risk level, and whether the sector is open to foreign capital. The wrong KBLI can hold up licensing.

Must agreements with foreign partners be in Indonesian?

Agreements involving an Indonesian party must be in Indonesian, and may be made bilingual. We draft bilingual Indonesian–English agreements so both parties understand the content.

How long does it take to set up a PT?

It depends on how complete the documents are, the type of business, and the licences required. We give you an estimated timeline once we understand your business plan.
